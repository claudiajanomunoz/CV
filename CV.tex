\section*{Research interests}

\noindent I use high-precision, ground-based near-infrared photometry to find and characterise planets around the coolest stars, working across the whole chain from detector to demographics. During my PhD I optimised the calibration and reduction pipeline of SPIRIT, an InGaAs CMOS detector at SPECULOOS-South, and characterised its long-term on-sky performance; this work underpins the validation of new planets such as TOI-2407\,b, a warm Neptune in the Neptune desert. I apply the same photometry to stellar and substellar variability, and I lead a collaboration combining MeerKAT radio and SPECULOOS infrared monitoring of brown-dwarf magnetospheres. Building on earlier machine-learning work on photoevaporation and the radius valley, I aim to connect instrument-limited precision to population questions, in particular the Neptune desert.
